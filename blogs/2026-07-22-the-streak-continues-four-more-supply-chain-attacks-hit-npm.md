Between early June and July 14, four more supply chain attacks hit npm and PyPI: a Shai-Hulud worm variant, typosquatted payment SDKs, a stolen publishing token, and a hijacked CI pipeline. Different entry points, one target: the credentials in developer environments and build pipelines.
